# Publication options after the first pilot

Checked against organizer calls on October 21, 2026. **Planning recommendation: consider ACL's January ARR cycle or LangCode in February.** These are alternatives to the November SAFE deadline, not commitments to submit or predictions of acceptance.

## ACL 2027 main conference and Findings

The [official call](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/) gives **January 4, 2027, 11:59 p.m. AoE** for ARR submission. Conference commitment follows reviews; its date remains TBA. Agents, discourse/pragmatics, semantics, and code models are relevant topics. This is the stronger research-paper target if the experiment produces a substantive result. Long and short papers are possible; main-track submissions also receive Findings consideration.

## LangCode 2027 at NAACL

The [workshop call](https://langcode2027.github.io/) includes coding-agent safety, output auditing, and trustworthiness. Its [submission page](https://langcode2027.github.io/submission/) gives **February 5, 2027, 11:59 p.m. AoE** for direct submissions and March 12 for commitment of already ARR-reviewed papers. This is a particularly natural workshop option for a focused code-review study. Accepted papers are archival. The published dates are subject to change.

## NAACL 2027 System Demonstrations

The [demo call](https://2027.naacl.org/calls/system_demonstration/) gives **December 4, 2026, 11:59 p.m. AoE**. Consider this only if the contribution becomes a reusable, meaningfully evaluated tool. It requires an accessible working system, a paper, and a short demonstration video. It prohibits simultaneous review at ARR and other venues, so it is not a parallel backup for the January route.

## SAFE and prospective workshop routes

[SAFE at AAAI 2027](https://www.safeworkshop.org/aaai-2027/) remains relevant, but its November 2 deadline should not determine the pace of an unrun model study. Consider it only if the pilot and writing are ready on their merits.

The [ICLR 2027 workshop call](https://www.iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForWorkshops) suggests February 1 for contributions, but individual workshop acceptance and calls are still pending. This is a prospective route, not a confirmed paper deadline for this project.

Choose a venue after the pilot and check its current originality and concurrent-submission rules. These options are sequential alternatives; the same paper should not be submitted to them in parallel where their rules prohibit it.
