## Concrete use of this map

1. Choose comparison objectives and reproduce accessible baselines before claiming a new mechanism.
2. Keep exact paper versions, artifact commits and policy assumptions with run records.
3. Have scientific reviewers examine the construct and scorer; let the project owner implement tools, fixtures, adapters and traces.
4. Use the map to read and compare work. Any outreach or job-related claims require a separate explicit task and fresh verification.
