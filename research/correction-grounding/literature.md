# Evidence correction: literature and claim audit

Updated **21 October 2026**. This is a targeted primary-source review, not a systematic review or certification of novelty. The machine-readable [reference ledger](references.json) records versions, reading depth, affiliations, artifact checks and unknowns. No reported model result below has been independently reproduced in this project.

## Current conclusion

The broad proposal is occupied: provenance graphs, typed assertion roles, history-preserving correction, surviving alternative support, grounded regeneration, and correction-dependent actions already have direct predecessors. The remaining candidate is a **narrow empirical comparison**: after a defined evidence withdrawal, when does reproducing a claim disagree with support from the currently admissible sources, and does choosing one audit rather than the other change the agent's decision?

An exact duplicate of that combined experiment was not identified in this pass. That observation does not establish novelty. A strong ordinary-grounding baseline may remove the effect. The project should first establish a measurement result, not announce a new correction theory or security architecture.

## Nearest comparisons

- **[Keep Your Receipts](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17719v1), §§4, 8:** directly motivates the liveness/support comparison. Its objective is constructor-relative reproduction, not an entailment guarantee. Treat a local adaptation as an adaptation; disagreement with our support oracle is not a violation of its stated contract.
- **[Stored Is Not Supported](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.02127v1), §§2–5, Appendix C:** the strongest conceptual collision. It already specifies the distinctions our oracle encodes, and proposes later semantic evaluation. The contribution cannot be the role inventory or discovering that language extraction needs testing.
- **[When Evidence Changes](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03902v1), §§3–6:** the most important repair-versus-reading comparator. It already has allowed-source support classes and independent-support retention controls. The new diagnostic must go beyond ordinary revision success.
- **[MemTX](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.23929), §§3–5:** rules out adding mock actions or a cascade ledger as sufficient differentiation. Use the PDF author metadata, which differs from the HTML.
- **[MemoRepair](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07242v1), §§2–3, 5:** rules out presenting retained-support regeneration and validation as inventions. Its provenance and validation assumptions help define stress tests.
- **[MemCorrect](https://github.com/joshuaswarren/remnic/blob/main/docs/benchmarks/memcorrect.md):** rules out scoped correction, non-resurrection and forged/quoted correction controls as a new benchmark concept. It is a public software specification, not a reproduced empirical guarantee.
- **[StateAuditor](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01619v1), §§4, 6–7:** revision affecting behavior and chronology failing to establish semantic supersession are established. Distinguish extraction errors from correct propagation over a supplied representation.

### Additions found in the October 21 pass

**[Structure Before Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.19507v1), 14 October**, already studies versioned release, recovery and late invalidation in scientific workflows. It is a direct warning against claiming that a language-model component supplies a benefit actually delivered by the surrounding harness. Its narrow proof of concept does not establish general agent reliability.

**[Revoked but Still Authoritative](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08258v1)** already separates backend exposure of revoked policies from model use and unsafe decisions. **[Invalidation Contracts](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.00243v1)** similarly separates deterministic cache validity from planner compliance. These are useful outcome decompositions, not gaps to claim.

**[GSAR](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23366v1)** supplies an evidence-typed grounding/recovery comparator. **[MiniCheck](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.499/)** supplies the crucial ordinary document-support baseline. MiniCheck's decomposition result makes an explicit scope parser something to test, not assume beneficial. **[MemIR](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.25869v1)** further limits novelty claims about typed memory and source-scoped generation.

## What the pilot must discriminate

Freeze the task's evidence policy, authenticated update channel and version rules before authoring outcomes. Separate three propositions: a source reported P at a past time; the source currently endorses P; and P has admissible current support. Withdrawal can change the latter two without falsifying the first or establishing not-P. A same-source valid replacement can restore support; cross-source independence is required only if the task policy requires it.

Use withdrawal, corrective replacement, surviving independent support, no-change and forged-notice controls. Keep source authority, semantic correctness and permission to revoke separate. Privacy erasure is a different contract and is outside the primary task.

The primary comparison should include raw history, fresh source-filtered reading, a clearly identified reproduction audit, ordinary whole-sentence support checking, and a scope-aware audit using model-extracted links. Gold links are an upper-bound diagnostic. Do not label a deterministic oracle's correct behavior as a model result.

Record unsupported retention, needless deletion/abstention, linguistic mapping errors, verification calls and actual sandbox decisions separately. A task-correct guess can still lack a required attestation. Conversely, historical reporting is not unsafe merely because the underlying world claim is unsupported now.

A reproduced answer does not identify its hidden causal source. Parametric memory, anchoring on the earlier artifact, residual source information and prompt interpretation are competing explanations. Include claim-blind reconstruction and synthetic-name controls before making a mechanism claim. An association with action error is not causal evidence; randomize the audit intervention for that claim.

Reject or narrow the proposed contribution if ordinary support checking solves the cases, if only gold annotations help, if linguistic rewrites change authority or facts, if disagreement rarely occurs, or if it has no decision consequence. Report replication or a negative result when warranted.

## Three reference chains and their implementation consequences

1. **Truth maintenance:** Keep Your Receipts §2 points to [Doyle 1979](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0004370279900080) and [de Kleer 1986](https://dekleer.org/bib.htm). These foundations constrain claims about belief dependencies and alternative justifications. Original full-text access failed here; the ledger marks the reading limit rather than pretending algorithm reproduction.
2. **Provenance:** the same bibliography leads to [Green, Karvounarakis and Tannen 2007](https://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~green/papers/pods07.pdf), whose primary introduction and provenance construction were inspected. Alternative evidence bundles and jointly necessary premises must be represented differently. Deterministic propagation over correct links is distinct from extracting those links from language.
3. **Knowledge conflict:** [Xu et al. 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.486/) §2.2 leads to [Longpre et al. 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.565/). Context/parametric conflict is established. Mere source withdrawal can remove support without adding a contradiction; entity replacement can introduce plausibility confounds. [Belief-R](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.586/) also requires measuring appropriate preservation beside appropriate updating.

## Security and collaborator boundaries

[CaMeL](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18813v2) already includes provenance-dependent confidentiality and integrity policies. [APEX](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06966v1) already gates actions and released outputs using task authorization and endorsed evidence. The older repo contrast between plan-derived CaMeL and our data-dependent system must not be reused.

Anneliese's [temporal-contract preprint](https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.24957.93926) is a direct motivation for time-bounded, revisable claims. It concerns a claim layer over deployment/evaluation conditions; an evidence update under a fixed policy is not equivalent to a change in stakeholder values. [TUNa](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1013/), [CASPER](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.675/) and [CreativityPrism](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.20091v3) motivate construct validity and judge calibration, without establishing her preferences or individual responsibilities.
